# E-commerce Sales & Customer Analysis

## Overview
Full stack analysis of E-commerce sales and customer data 
using Python, Pandas, Matplotlib and Power BI to uncover 
revenue trends, top products, and customer patterns.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Power BI
- JupyterLab

## Questions Answered
- Which country generates the most revenue?
- Which products sell the most?
- Which month had the highest sales?
- Who are the top 10 customers?
- What is the average order value?
- How has revenue grown over time?

## Key Findings
- Total revenue across all transactions was 
  $8,911,407.90.

- The United Kingdom generated the most revenue 
  with $7,308,391.55 accounting for over 82% 
  of total global sales.

- Paper Craft Little Birdie was the best selling 
  product with 80,995 units sold followed by 
  Medium Ceramic Top Storage Jar with 77,916 units.

- November 2011 was the highest revenue month 
  with $1,161,817.38 in sales.

- The top customer generated $280,206.02 in revenue 
  showing a small group of customers contribute 
  disproportionately to total sales.

- The average order value across all transactions 
  was $480.87.
