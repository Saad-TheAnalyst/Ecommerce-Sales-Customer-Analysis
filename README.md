# E-commerce Sales & Customer Analysis Dashboard

## Overview
A comprehensive full-stack analysis of 397,884 
e-commerce transactions using Python, Pandas, 
Matplotlib and Power BI to uncover revenue trends, 
customer lifetime value, RFM segmentation and 
sales patterns across multiple countries.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- NumPy
- Power BI
- JupyterLab

## Questions Answered
- Which country generates the most revenue?
- Who are the most valuable customers?
- What time of day has the most sales?
- Which products sell the most?
- How are customers segmented by value?
- What is the average customer lifetime value?
- How does revenue trend month by month?
- What is each country's market share?

## Key Findings

### Overall Performance
- Total revenue across all transactions was $8,911,407.
- Total unique customers: 4,338
- Average customer lifetime value: $2,054.27
- Highest customer lifetime value: $280,206.02

### RFM Customer Segmentation
- Average customer recency: 93 days since last purchase
- Average order frequency: 4.3 orders per customer
- VIP customers — spending over $10,000 — represent 
  a small but critical revenue segment
- Low value customers spending under $500 make up 
  the majority of the customer base

### Customer Lifetime Value
- Top customer generated $280,206.02 in lifetime revenue
- Customer 14911 placed 201 orders making them the 
  most frequent buyer
- Average order value across all customers is $480.87
- Top 10 customers contribute disproportionately 
  to total revenue

### Hourly Sales Pattern
- Peak sales hour is 12:00 noon with $1,378,571 
  in revenue
- Sales are highest between 9:00 AM and 3:00 PM
- Very little activity before 8:00 AM and after 
  8:00 PM showing this is a B2B focused business

### Country Market Share
- United Kingdom dominates with 82.01% market share
- Netherlands comes second with 3.20%
- Germany is third with 2.57%
- Top 5 countries account for 93.11% of all revenue

### Monthly Revenue Trend
- November 2011 was the best month with $1,161,817
- September and October 2011 also performed strongly
- Revenue shows clear seasonality with Q4 being 
  the strongest quarter

### Basket Size
- Average items per order: 278.9 items
- Average order value: $480.87
- Largest single order contained 80,995 items 
  confirming this is a wholesale business
